## Project Overview

Scissors is a premium hair and beauty salon website designed to deliver an elegant digital
experience for clients seeking personalised beauty and self-care services. The platform
combines sophisticated visual design, service discovery, appointment scheduling, and brand
storytelling to create a seamless salon booking
experience.![Scissors — Luxury Salon Hero](/images/projects/HairSalon/hero-hair.png)

The website showcases the salon's services, experience, customer trust, and luxury
positioning while making it easy for visitors to explore treatments and book appointments.

## Core Features

### 1. Luxury Landing Page

- Premium salon branding and visual presentation.
- Full-screen hero section with professional salon imagery.
- Prominent appointment booking call-to-action.
- Customer trust indicators and experience highlights.
- Elegant typography and luxury-focused design.### 2. Service Showcase

![Scissors — Luxury Service Catalogue](/images/projects/HairSalon/luxury-hair.png)

- **Skin Care** — Beauty and skincare treatments.
- **Styling** — Personalised hairstyling services.
- **Hair Treatment** — Specialised hair treatments.### 3. Online Appointment Booking

![Scissors — Appointment Booking](/images/projects/HairSalon/about-hair.png)

- Dedicated appointment booking interface.
- Interactive calendar for date selection.
- Available time-slot presentation.
- Multiple appointment time options.
- Streamlined booking experience.### 4. Salon Gallery

- Dedicated section introducing the salon.

- Dedicated testimonials section.

The website follows a luxury-focused visual direction, combining professional salon
photography, elegant layouts, and intuitive navigation. Its responsive interface
prioritises service discovery and appointment booking while maintaining a consistent
premium brand identity.
